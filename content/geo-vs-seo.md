Today I learned that ranking first on Google no longer guarantees visibility in the AI era, leading to a shift toward GEO (Generative Engine Optimization). Traditional SEO focuses on keyword density and backlinks to win clicks, while generative engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity synthesize answers directly. Because of this, typical SEO-optimized sites often miss out on GenAI responses since these models rely on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to find structured, authoritative data.

Optimizing for GEO is mostly about making content easy for AI to trust and cite. This usually means using clear Q&A formats, sharing original data or research, and establishing a presence on niche forums and databases. When the information is easy to verify, these models are much more likely to pull it into their synthesized answers.
